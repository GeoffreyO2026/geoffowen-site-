Working with Me

This work begins with
a conversation,
not a programme.

I created Biologically Aligned Parenting after nearly forty years witnessing the ineffectiveness of modern parenting, and wanting to help parents reboot their biology and get back to raising healthy, confident children in a way that gives them more time, less stress, and more energy to actually enjoy life. That was my own experience raising two daughters, applying the same principles I now teach. Modern parenting treats parenting as a role or a job to be managed. But parenting is not a role or a job. It is an ancient, biological responsibility to provide the optimum environment for a child to mature, the same as anywhere else in the animal kingdom. Biology never left us. It is still running underneath everything, constantly reading the environment for safety and certainty. When that is present, a child settles. When it is not, the behaviour that follows is not defiance. It is biology doing exactly what it was built to do.

What follows is not a set of tips or tricks. It is closer to a reboot, a return to what was already there before modern parenting moved away from it. If that is something you want for your own children, here is how we might work together.

You're not a bad parent.
The Turning Point Session

There are moments, for many parents, when something in you reacts in a way you would rather it had not. Perhaps it happens at the end of a long day, perhaps somewhere else entirely, but you know it is not who you are, and not knowing why it keeps happening can weigh almost as much as the moment itself. This is not the start of a programme. It is a single, focused session to look at your child's behaviour through a different lens, and your own, so what has actually been going on becomes clear.

This is not a diagnosis of your child, and it is not a fix for everything. It is a conversation to discover what is actually driving your reactions toward your child, and how to bring greater calm when there feels like there is little left to give.

Reactive → Calm
Confused → Clear
Guilt-ridden → Understanding
Avoidant → Certain
Exhausted → Energised
Disconnected → Close

Reactive to Calm. Where before a small thing could tip you into a reaction you regretted, you find yourself able to see the moment coming and respond to it rather than being pulled along by it.

Confused to Clear. Where before your child's behaviour felt unpredictable or impossible to make sense of, you begin to read it as a signal, and know what it is actually telling you.

Guilt-ridden to Understanding. Where before every difficult moment left you wondering what you had done wrong, you start to see what was actually happening, and the guilt that came from not knowing begins to lift.

Avoidant to Certain. Where before you found yourself putting off the harder conversations or the harder moments, you know what is required of you and are able to meet it directly.

Exhausted to Energised. Where before parenting felt like something that drained you further every day, it stops costing you what it used to, and some of what it takes begins to come back.

Disconnected to Close. Where before you and your child felt like you were moving further apart, you find your way back toward each other, and the relationship starts to feel like one again.
