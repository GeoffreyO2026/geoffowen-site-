A different starting point

Understanding what drives the encounter

Much modern parenting advice begins with stopping, correcting or managing behaviour. BAP begins from the position that children are born exquisitely social, with innate expectations of their experience with the adults upon whom they depend. We examine what your child encounters through you, how protective responses enter an exchange and what can become different in your way of responding.

We can take hurtful words and practical boundaries seriously while examining what is driving the pattern. The aim is a specific change in everyday family life. Simply becoming quiet or compliant is not our sole measure of progress.

Personal attention over three months

What is included

Your onboarding session

Before onboarding, complete the two preparation forms, My Family and What’s Going On. We look at your family’s circumstances, what you have tried, what has helped and what has not produced the change you wanted. We examine why those approaches have been unsuccessful and compare their assumptions with the BAP perspective. Together, we identify a particular recurring behaviour pattern and agree the shift we will work towards.

Your weekly sessions

Onboarding is the first of your 12 sessions. The remaining 11 sessions provide guidance and direction as you recognise the pattern, explore the distinctions and characteristics of BAP, and respond differently. We work with what is actually happening at home, using the guide What Your Child’s Behaviour Might Be Telling You (BAP) alongside our conversations.

Support between sessions
  • WhatsApp with me. For urgent parenting guidance Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm UK time, following GMT/BST. Messages received during those hours receive a same-day response. Messages outside those hours are answered on the next working day. This is not an emergency service.
  • The BAP AI support tool. Guidance, reminders and help revisiting the distinctions between sessions, including after hours. It can make mistakes and does not replace my personal guidance or emergency help.
  • Additional sessions. Programme clients can book additional 30-minute sessions at £100, compared with the standalone guidance rate of £140 for 30 minutes.
My commitment

A specific, agreed behaviour shift

At onboarding, we agree what the shift will look like in a particular recurring situation. I continue working with you until that specific, agreed shift is achieved. The commitment concerns the shift we identify together; it is not a promise to remove every expression of anger or every difficulty in family life.
